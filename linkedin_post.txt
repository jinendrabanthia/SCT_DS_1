I am excited to share the completion of my first task (SCT_DS_1) as a Data Science Intern at SkillCraft Technology! 🚀

For this task, I created a data visualization showing the Top 10 Most Populous Countries in 2024. I used a horizontal bar chart to effectively display the distribution of population across these nations.

To make the chart clean and readable, I converted the raw population numbers (originally in thousands) into millions and utilized a modern color palette.

🛠️ Tools Used:
- Python 🐍
- Pandas for data manipulation 🐼
- Matplotlib & Seaborn for data visualization 📊

You can check out the generated chart and my code in my GitHub repository!
